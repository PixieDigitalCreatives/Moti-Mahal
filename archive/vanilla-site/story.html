The Moti Mahal story

1947 Craft Today

Chapter one · The first fire

A tandoor in Old Delhi

The Moti Mahal story began in 1947 in Daryaganj, Old Delhi, around a clay tandoor that would change how India ate. Chicken marinated in yoghurt and spice met the searing heat of charcoal — and tandoori cooking found its home at the restaurant table.

Chapter two · The classics

Butter, tomato and patience

From that fire came the dishes the Moti Mahal name is known for: butter chicken, rich with tomato and white butter, and dal makhani, black lentils left to simmer through the night. We still cook them the long way — there is no shortcut to that depth.

Chapter three · Jaypee Greens

A new home, the same fire

In Sun Twilight, at the heart of Jaypee Greens, our kitchen carries those recipes forward for a new neighbourhood — the same marinades, the same hand-ground masalas, the same charcoal. Pull up a chair; you are family here.
